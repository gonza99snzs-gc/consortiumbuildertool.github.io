Consortium builder. Add the companies that have shown interest, then drag them into the consortiums. You can add up to 10 corridors, and 5 need to close. Each consortium needs one confirmed company per workstream (2–5). Use the "Possible" box under each slot for companies you are still considering.

1. Add interested companies

Type the company name, choose its workstream and level of interest, then press Enter or Add. Interest: 3 = fully engaged (thick outline, bold text), 2 = interested (thin outline), 1 = not fully engaged yet but worth mapping (dashed outline). Click the dots on any company to change its rating later.
